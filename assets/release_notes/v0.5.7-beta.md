## New in this release

### New
- **Favourite classes and teachers** in Browse timetables: tap the star next to a class or teacher (or in the top bar of its timetable) and it shows at the top of its list under "Favourites". Tap the star again to remove it. Favourites are kept per account on this device.

### Fixed
- Custom homework, exams and lesson details: the note "Only for you, not sent to the school." under the text field now wraps onto a second line on small screens instead of being cut off with "…". The hint under the account name wraps too.
