## New in this release

### New
- Add your own homework to a lesson, like your own exams. Your own homework and exams now also appear in the Info center, marked “Custom”.

### Changed
- Turning on sync first checks whether the account already syncs (e.g. on another device). If it does, you only confirm, or enter the existing passphrase if it has one, instead of being asked to set sync up again.
- The first time you open **Teachers** in the timetables menu, a notice asks you not to use teacher timetables to contact teachers in their free periods and points you to the office hours. A period that looks free often isn't (hall duty, meetings). It's shown once per device.
