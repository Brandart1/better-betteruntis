## New in this release

### New
- **Web version:** BetterBetterUntis now runs in your browser at **https://better-betteruntis.eu**, nothing to install. Its menu has a *Get the app* link to the Android and iOS apps.
- **Sync (optional):** sync your custom exams, lesson details and settings between your phone, tablet and the web version (*Menu → Sync*). Settings include lesson and subject colours, text options and all accessibility settings. Sync is off until you turn it on; turning it off deletes everything stored on the server, while your devices keep their notes. The web version can sync one account; the apps can sync several.
- **End-to-end encryption for sync (optional, recommended):** protect synced data with a sync passphrase and a recovery code. The server then only stores encrypted data it can't read. In the web version you choose how long the browser remembers the key.
- **Custom exams:** in a lesson's details, tap *Mark as exam* to turn it into an exam, then optionally enter details such as "mündliche Wiederholung" or the exam topics. *Remove custom exam* undoes it.
- **Your lesson details:** add or edit your own details for any lesson (*Add details*). They show in the timetable's detailed view, the lesson details, exports and synced calendars.
- **Add to calendar** (Android and iOS): in the export sheet, choose *Calendar (.ics)* and tap *Add to calendar*. For regular lessons, substitutions, exams and cancelled lessons you pick a new calendar (coloured like your timetable settings), one of your own calendars, or *Don't add*. The next 2–12 weeks are added and kept up to date whenever you open the app: substitutions, room changes and cancellations update the events, removed lessons disappear. Manage or stop syncs under *Menu → Calendar sync*. *Download .ics* is still there for a one-off file.
- **Export to your calendar:** the export sheet offers **Calendar (.ics)**. A specific week becomes one event per lesson (cancelled lessons are marked, holidays are all-day events); the regular timetable becomes weekly repeating events until the end of the school year.
- **What's new:** after an update, the app shows what changed since the version you used last (also when you skipped releases). Reread it anytime under *Menu → What's new*; it works offline.
- **Keyboard:** with a hardware keyboard (computer, tablet), ← / → switch weeks (or days in the day view), Home or T jumps to today, and Escape goes back from the day view to the week. In the web version, ‹ and › buttons next to *Today* do the same.

### Improved
- The export button is always visible in the timetable's top bar instead of hidden in the ⋮ menu.
- Past days, the part of today that is over and lessons that have ended are greyed out much more clearly.
- Calendar sync (Android): clearer setup for Google calendars, own calendars inside a Google account are listed too, a refresh button, and a button to delete calendars that only exist on this phone.

### Fixed
- "Browse timetables": the Classes / Teachers / Rooms tabs are centred again (#2).
- Lessons without a subject but with a custom title (e.g. "E-Theater") are exported with that title instead of "Lesson" (#3).

### Changed
- The self-hosting server (Docker image and `…-web-linux-x64.tar.gz`) has been removed. If you still want to host the web version yourself, use a 0.4.x release.
- Privacy: the web version and sync are described in the [privacy notice](https://github.com/Brandart1/better-betteruntis/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md).
