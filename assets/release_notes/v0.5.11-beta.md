## New in this release

### New
- **Notifications** (Settings → Notifications, per account, off by default): substitutions, cancellations and room changes in your timetable for today and the next school days, and new messages. Android also checks in the background; iOS checks when you open the app; in the browser they show inside the app. Everything stays on your device: the app asks WebUntis itself with your login.
- **Daily summary**: every day at a time you choose (07:00 by default), one notification with today's changes and tests, your own tests included. Changes found after that are notified right away. Android checks every 15 minutes on school days from an hour before that time until 18:00 (on Android 14+, "Allow exact time" makes the summary come on the minute).
- **Reminders** before tests and homework: choose how many days before (1–7, off by default); they come at the daily time. Your own tests and homework count too.
- With sync on, the **notification options** (kinds, days ahead, daily time, summary and reminders) sync per account to your other devices. Turning notifications on or off stays per device, since each device has to allow them.
