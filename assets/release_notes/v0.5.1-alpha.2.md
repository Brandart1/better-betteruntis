## New in this release

### Improved
- **Large text** (system font size and/or the app's text size) works much better on phones: switches move below their labels instead of breaking words letter by letter (e.g. "Voice language", "Log in anonymously"), the Browse tabs scroll instead of being cut off, the week's day numbers are no longer clipped ("1" for 16), the date in the top bar shortens to fit, and the login screen no longer overflows. Lesson cards leave out lines that don't fit instead of showing them cut in half.
- System and app text size together are capped at 250 %, and Android's own scaling (big text grows a little less) is kept.
